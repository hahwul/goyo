`codepen`

- `codepen`: Embeds a CodePen pen. It takes `user` and `pen_id` as required parameters, and optionally `default_tab` (defaults to `result`).

```jinja
{% raw %}{{< codepen user="hahwul" pen_id="ByKwxwd" />}}{% endraw %}
```
